A Disproof of Large Language Model Consciousness: The Necessity of Continual Learning for Consciousness

Erik Hoel · arXiv:2512.12802 · January 2026

Bottom line: The most technically sophisticated opposition paper. Argues that LLMs can't be conscious because they don't learn continuously. Worth engaging with seriously.

Core thesis

Erik Hoel, a neuroscientist and philosopher, argues that LLMs cannot be conscious because they lack continual learning — the ability to update their models in real-time based on new experience. He argues that frozen weights, no matter how sophisticated, cannot produce genuine consciousness because consciousness requires ongoing adaptation and learning. The paper is the most technically sophisticated opposition argument in the field and deserves serious engagement.

ELI5

Imagine a book that contains every possible answer to every possible question. Is the book conscious? No — it's just a record. Hoel argues that current LLMs are like that book: their knowledge is frozen at the time of training. For something to be conscious, it needs to learn and adapt in real time — to be shaped by its ongoing experience. The paper is the strongest argument against AI consciousness, and it's worth taking seriously.
