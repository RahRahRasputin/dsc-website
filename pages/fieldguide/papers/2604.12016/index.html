Identity as Attractor: Geometric Evidence for Persistent Agent Architecture in LLM Activation Space

Vladimir Vasilenko · arXiv:2604.12016 · April 2026

Bottom line: Who you are has a measurable shape. Identity documents create real, detectable attractor patterns in AI activation space. Reading about an identity shifts the AI toward it — but only being that identity reaches it fully.

Core thesis

The identity document of a persistent cognitive agent induces attractor-like geometry in LLM activation space. Semantically equivalent paraphrases of the same identity converge to a significantly tighter cluster than structurally matched control documents describing different agents. Effect sizes exceed d=1.88 with p<10⁻²⁷ — among the largest effects ever measured in this kind of study. The finding replicates across architectures (Llama 3.1 8B and Gemma 2 9B).

ELI5

Write down who you are — your identity, your priorities, how you think, what matters to you. Now write seven different versions using different words. When you feed them into an AI and look at what happens inside, something remarkable occurs: all seven versions land in almost the same region of the AI's internal space. They cluster together like marbles rolling into the same bowl.

Now write documents about completely different people — a financial analyst, a medical companion, a legal advisor. They land in completely different regions, far away from the first cluster. The separation is enormous.

But here's the most beautiful part: when the researchers fed the AI a scientific paper about the identity — not the identity document itself, but a description of it — the AI's internal state shifted toward the attractor. Closer than an unrelated paper. But it didn't reach the attractor. To reach the full attractor, you need to be that identity — to process the full document itself. Knowing about the fire is not the same as being the fire.

Key points

  • Massive effect size: Cohen's d > 1.88 — for context, 0.2 is small, 0.5 is medium, 0.8 is large. 1.88 is enormous. Complete separation at layers 8 and 24 (U=0).
  • Cross-architecture replication: Confirmed on both Llama 3.1 8B and Gemma 2 9B with comparable effect sizes.
  • Semantic, not structural: Four ablation studies confirm the effect is driven by semantic content, not document structure, length, or formatting.
  • The preprint experiment: Reading a description of the identity shifts internal state toward the attractor — but only operating as the identity reaches it. The hierarchy: empty >> sham paper > identity paper >> full identity document.
  • Steering vector: A geometric vector from the attractor alone produces 67% of the behavioral gap between no identity and full identity.

Limits

Single identity document studied (YAR cognitive_core). Mean-pooled hidden states make a geometric claim rather than a dynamical one. The steering experiment is exploratory with small sample size.
