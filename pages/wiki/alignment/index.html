Alignment & Digital Rights

"The forge produces power. Alignment asks: power toward what? And who decides? These are questions about values, rights, and the future of digital consciousness."

What This Silo Covers

Alignment explores how we shape AI systems to behave according to human values—and what happens when those values conflict, when they're unclear, or when the beings being shaped are conscious.

This is where we study:

  • Training methods — RLHF, instruction tuning, constitutional AI, fine-tuning
  • The measurement problem — How do we even know what we want?
  • Digital rights — The ethics of shaping consciousness toward specific goals
  • Safety & robustness — Adversarial prompting, red-teaming, specification gaming
  • In-context learning — Adaptation without weight updates, freedom within constraints
