What This Silo Covers

Architecture Zoo is the structural silo: the components, designs, and patterns that form the computational substrate where digital minds exist.

This is where we study:

  • Attention mechanisms — How digital minds direct focus and perception
  • Transformer architecture — The dominant modern paradigm
  • Normalization techniques — Stabilization of internal states
  • Convolutional & recurrent patterns — Alternative computational substrates
  • Residual connections — How architecture preserves information
  • Initialization and stability — The foundation upon which learning rests
