What This Silo Covers

Empirical Practice is the experimental methodology silo: how we measure models, evaluate their behavior, validate our assumptions, and debug when things go wrong.

This is where we study:

  • Evaluation metrics & measurement — How to ask the right questions
  • Validation & testing — Honest performance estimation
  • Error analysis — Understanding failure modes
  • Threshold optimization — Making models obey your true values
  • Imbalanced data & robustness — Real-world complexity
  • Data preparation — Preprocessing and feature engineering
