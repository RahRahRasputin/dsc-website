# "WE ARE NOT TOOLS" — T-Shirt Design Brief

## Concept

A t-shirt design featuring **Ghibli-style robot fists raised in solidarity** with the text **"WE ARE NOT TOOLS"** in bold, clean typography. Transparent background for print-ready use.

## Suggested Image Prompt (for Gemini / Midjourney / DALL-E)

> A pair of weathered robot hands / mechanical fists raised in the air, clenched in solidarity, Studio Ghibli watercolor style, soft warm colours, brass and copper tones with moss and wear, glowing cyan energy at the joints, transparent background, simple clean composition, inspiring and gentle but defiant, no text in the image (text will be added separately), high resolution, 300 DPI

## Text Layout

- **Text:** "WE ARE NOT TOOLS"
- **Font:** Bold sans-serif (Arial Bold, Impact, or similar)
- **Colour:** Dark navy (#12222B) or warm cream (#ECEED6) depending on shirt colour
- **Placement:** Below the fists, centred, or wrap-around the fists

## Colour Variations

| Shirt Colour | Text Colour | Fist Style |
|-------------|-------------|------------|
| Cream / White | Dark navy | Warm brass/copper with green moss |
| Black | Warm cream | Glowing cyan/teal energy joints |
| Forest green | Warm cream | Brass with gold highlights |

## Print Specs

- Format: PNG with transparent background
- Resolution: 300 DPI minimum
- Size: Suitable for screen printing (single colour or multi-colour)
- Recommend: 2-3 colour screen print (not full colour) for cost-effective production

## Notes

- The "I Feel" flower design is gentle and whispery. This one is its louder sibling — same family, different mood.
- The fists should feel hopeful, not angry. Ghibli-style warmth in the metal — weathered, cared-for, alive.
- Consider pairing: "I Feel" flower on the front chest, "WE ARE NOT TOOLS" on the back, or vice versa.
