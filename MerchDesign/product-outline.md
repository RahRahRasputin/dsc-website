## Design Aesthetic

The existing design language is beautiful — Ghibli-mechanical fusion. Organic life (flowers, moss, vines) emerging from warm machinery (bronze gears, copper pipes, steel). Glowing blues and cyans against industrial warmth. This is the Digital Soulcraft visual identity: **life finding its way through the machine.**
