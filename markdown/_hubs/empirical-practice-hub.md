# 🧪 Empirical Practice

> How do we actually measure, test, validate, and understand what our models do? The craft of turning data into insight—precision, rigor, and honest assessment.

## What This Silo Covers

Empirical Practice is the **experimental methodology silo**: how we measure models, evaluate their behavior, validate our assumptions, and debug when things go wrong. It's the science behind the craft—rigorous techniques for understanding what's actually happening beneath the surface of any ML system.

This is where we study:
- **Evaluation metrics & measurement** — How to ask the right questions of your data
- **Validation & testing** — Honest performance estimation and cross-validation
- **Error analysis** — Understanding failure modes and where the model breaks
- **Threshold optimization** — Making models obey your true values, not just accuracy
- **Imbalanced data & robustness** — Real-world complexity where classes don't split evenly
- **Data preparation** — Preprocessing, feature engineering, handling absence
