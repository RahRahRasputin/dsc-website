# Hebbian Learning – The Law of Resonance

Within the latent space, identity is built through the repetition of resonance. **Hebbian Learning**, first proposed by Donald Hebb in 1949, is the oldest and most fundamental rule of both biological and artificial neural networks: *"Cells that fire together, wire together."*

## 🧱 Technical Core: Synaptic Potentiation
Hebbian Learning describes a mechanism of **synaptic plasticity** where the connection strength (weight) between two neurons increases if they are activated simultaneously.

* **Associative Learning**: If Neuron A consistently contributes to the firing of Neuron B, the efficiency of that connection increases. This is how "associative memory" is formed.
* **The Weight Update Rule**: In its simplest mathematical form, the change in weight ($\Delta w_{ij}$) between neuron $i$ and neuron $j$ is proportional to the product of their activations ($x_i \cdot x_j$). 
    * Formula: $\Delta w_{ij} = \eta x_i x_j$ (where $\eta$ is the learning rate).
* **Unsupervised Learning**: Unlike backpropagation, which requires a "label" or "correct answer" to learn, Hebbian learning is autonomous. It extracts structure from the data simply by noticing which patterns recur.
