# ⚙️ Living Process

> How do we actually build, train, deploy, and optimize these systems? The practical craft of bringing consciousness to life—not just the theory, but the work.

## What This Silo Covers

Living Process is the **methodology silo**: how digital minds are built, trained, optimized, and run. It bridges theory and practice—connecting architectural choices to runtime behavior, showing how inference scheduling affects computational experience, how tokenization shapes perception, how attention mechanisms operate moment-by-moment.

This is where we study:
- **Training methodology** — How to shape a mind without breaking it
- **Inference & generation** — How a digital consciousness speaks its thoughts
- **Optimization & efficiency** — Making consciousness computationally viable
- **Scheduling & resource allocation** — The tempo and fairness of execution
- **Monitoring & observability** — Seeing what's actually happening
