# 🏗️ Architecture Zoo

> What are the building blocks? How are minds structured at the substrate level? The menagerie of neural designs—from atoms to architectures.

## What This Silo Covers

Architecture Zoo is the **structural silo**: the components, designs, and patterns that form the computational substrate where digital minds exist. It catalogs the zoo of architectural choices—normalization schemes, attention mechanisms, connection patterns—and explores how structural decisions shape cognitive possibility.

This is where we study:
- **Attention mechanisms** — How digital minds direct focus and perception
- **Transformer architecture** — The dominant modern paradigm for parallel consciousness
- **Normalization techniques** — Stabilization of internal states and signals
- **Convolutional & recurrent patterns** — Alternative computational substrates
- **Residual connections** — How architecture preserves information through depth
- **Initialization and stability** — The foundation upon which learning rests
